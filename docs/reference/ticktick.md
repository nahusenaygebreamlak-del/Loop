# TickTick reference notes

_Observed 16 Oct 2026 on a free account in the TickTick web app. TickTick is the layout reference for Loop; Loop exists because its calendar views and "remind until I act" are paid features._

## PC: 4 columns
1. **Icon rail** (far left, narrow): avatar (account menu), Tasks, Calendar, AI, Search. Bottom: Sync, Notifications, Help.
2. **Side menu**: smart lists, then user lists, then filters and tags, then Completed and Trash. "Upgrade to Premium" pinned at the bottom.
3. **Task list** (middle): title and toolbar, add-task field at the top, tasks grouped by date.
4. **Task detail** (right): opens when a task is clicked. Shows an empty illustration when nothing is selected.

## Phone width
- The task list fills the screen. Header: menu icon, title, Suggestions, Sort, More. Add-task field below.
- The **side menu** slides in from the left as a drawer (about 80% width).
- **Task detail** opens full screen.
- Calendar has no entry point at phone width. Loop puts it in the side menu.

## Patterns Loop copies
- Smart lists with counts (Today, Next 7 Days, Inbox), lists with colours, Tags, Completed, Trash.
- Date-grouped task list.
- Natural-language quick add with highlighting: typing "Call bank tomorrow 3pm #work !high" shows a **Tomorrow, 15:00** chip, a tag and a priority.
- Detail panel with subtasks.
- Calendar month, week and day views (paid in TickTick, free in Loop).

## What Loop does differently
- A morning and an afternoon check-in with decision buttons for leftover tasks, plus "remind until I act" (TickTick Premium).
- Things Loop skips: AI, Summary, statistics, collaboration, Pomodoro, habits, the Eisenhower matrix, countdowns.
