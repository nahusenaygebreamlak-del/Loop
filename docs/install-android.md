# Installing Loop on your Android phone

Loop is installed directly, not from the Play Store. Each new version is a new APK file from an Expo build.

## First install

1. On your phone, open the build link Claude sends you (it looks like `https://expo.dev/accounts/nahu0024/projects/loop/builds/...`). You can also scan the QR code on that page with the phone's camera.
2. Tap **Install** to download the `.apk` file.
3. Android asks whether to allow installs from this source (Chrome or Files). Allow it **for that app only**. You can turn it off again afterwards in Settings › Apps › Special app access › Install unknown apps.
4. Open the downloaded file and tap **Install**. Google Play Protect may say it doesn't recognise the developer. That's because the app isn't from the Play Store. Choose **Install anyway**.
5. Open Loop and sign in with Google. Your tasks sync from your account.

## Updating

Install the newer APK over the old one, the same way. Your data stays: it's on the phone and in your account.

## If something goes wrong

- **"App not installed"**: uninstall the old Loop first, then install again. Your tasks come back from your account after you sign in.
- **Google sign-in returns to a blank screen**: close Loop fully and open it again; you should be signed in.
